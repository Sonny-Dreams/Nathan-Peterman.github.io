### Recent Work
Currently an electromechanical actuator engineer at [Stoke Space](https://www.stokespace.com/ "Stoke Space"). These actuators throttle and steer the engines on the Nova reusable rocket. You can see videos of engine hot fires on the [Stoke Space Linkedin or Twitter](https://x.com/stoke_space/status/2046938463283318858?s=20 "Stoke Space Linkedin or Twitter"). Former electrical engineer at [Agility Robotics](https://www.agilityrobotics.com/ "Agility Robotics"). Videos of our robots can be found on the [Agility Robotics' Youtube Channel](https://www.youtube.com/agilityrobotics "Agility Robotics' Youtube Channel"). I graduated from Purdue University in 2020 after interning at NASA Johnson Space Center and the National Institute of Standards and Technology. I plan on using this website to document personal projects and archive some research I have done into space and robotics.
