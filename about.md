### Recent Work
Currently an electromechanical actuator engineer at [Stoke Space](https://www.stokespace.com/ "Stoke Space"). These actuators throttle and steer the engines on the Nova reusable rocket. You can see videos of engine hot fires on the [Stoke Space Linkedin or Twitter](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/stoke-space_first-trim-test-of-our-zenith-ze12-engine-activity-7452704995545837568-K0P_ utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAADj6DlcB0hlKh2bkHlr1GtWxfRX7iGhXV-M "Stoke Space Linkedin or Twitter). Former electrical engineer at [Agility Robotics](https://www.agilityrobotics.com/ "Agility Robotics"). Videos of our robots can be found on the [Agility Robotics' Youtube Channel](https://www.youtube.com/agilityrobotics "Agility Robotics' Youtube Channel). I graduated from Purdue University in 2020 after interning at NASA Johnson Space Center and the National Institute of Standards and Technology. I plan on using this website to document personal projects and archive some research I have done into space and robotics.
